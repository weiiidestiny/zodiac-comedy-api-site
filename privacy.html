Privacy Policy

Zodiac Comedy uses social platform APIs to manage and publish content to accounts authorized by the account owner.

Information we access

The integration may access account/profile identifiers, publishing permissions, media, boards or other information required by the platform to provide publishing functionality, subject to permissions granted by the user.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts and create, schedule, manage, or publish Zodiac Comedy content through supported platform APIs.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the relevant platform or service providers when necessary to operate the publishing integration.

Data retention and access

Credentials and API data are retained only as needed to operate the integration and may be revoked through the applicable platform account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Zodiac Comedy account owner through the official Zodiac Comedy social profile.
